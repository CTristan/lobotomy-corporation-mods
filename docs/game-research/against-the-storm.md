# Against the Storm

- **Genre:** Roguelite settlement builder. You lead a company of mixed species out from the
  Smoldering City to found and stabilize one settlement per run under a hostile, resetting storm cycle.
- **Why a candidate:** It is the master map's second in-genre anchor (86), a commercially proven
  management roguelite that pairs with dotAGE, and its Approach 1 peak is the cleanest working proof
  that drafting your buildings and perks each run keeps a builder replayable, which is the exact
  lesson the conditioned-draft POC has to reproduce in-genre.
- **Confidence:** medium. The master map's read is high-confidence at the game level, but the
  POC-level detail (how much the blueprint and cornerstone *offer* is conditioned on your held state
  versus a flat pool the player supplies synergy for) is not yet observed firsthand.
- **Readiness:** researching

## Mechanic inventory *(relaxed)*

Seeded from the master map's existing read, to verify and deepen by play:
- **Blueprint + cornerstone draft.** Each settlement drafts its production buildings and cornerstone
  perks from randomized offers at fixed reputation milestones, so the systems available to you are a
  drafted loadout that recombines run to run rather than a fixed build list.
- **Imposed procedural conditions.** Every settlement rolls a biome, a starting-goods hand, a
  blueprint pool, and a set of glade events, so the content you manage is regenerated each run rather
  than re-skinned.
- **Glade events couple reward to telegraphed risk.** Cracking a forest glade offers a procedural
  reward gated behind a telegraphed hazard, so every roll states its stakes before you commit.
- **Three-clan mix (soft asymmetry).** The species you settle with each want different goods and
  carry different production strengths, so the clan mix tilts which drafted systems pay off.
- **Smoldering City meta-tree (across-run lever).** Persistent between-run upgrades widen what a
  settlement can start with and reach, which is the game's meta-progression lever.
- **Real-time storm/economy pressure.** Settlements run on a live economy under a resetting storm
  cycle, which is the pacing layer, and the part the master map flags as not transferring to
  LobCorp's day-by-day resolution.

## Approach mapping *(graduating)*

- **1 drafted loadout + interacting pieces:** peak (86). The blueprint and cornerstone offers
  recombine your available systems every settlement, which is the in-genre model for the abnormality
  draft the POC has to build, so this is the lever to study first.
- **2 procedural / affixed content:** strong (85). Biome, starting goods, blueprint pool, and glade
  events regenerate the content each run, and the glade-event system is the legible-affix bar, every
  procedural reward coupled to a telegraphed risk, that a rerolled abnormality has to clear.
- **3 asymmetric starts / factions:** soft, unslotted. The three-clan mix tilts a run without being a
  full "different kind" of start, and dotAGE's Elders already carry the in-genre proof for this lever.
- **4 meta-progression / unlock divergence:** present but redundant, unslotted. The Smoldering City
  tree is real cross-run progression, but dotAGE covers the in-genre meta-tree and Dead Cells covers
  pool-expansion more directly, so the master map leaves it out on redundancy, not on a cap.
- **5 structural run-restructuring:** partial, through the imposed biome and starting conditions, but
  the run shape (settle, hit reputation milestones, survive the storm) is fixed, so the variance is in
  contents and conditions rather than structure.
- **6 emergent systems + adaptive director:** not this game's lever. Pressure comes from the storm
  cycle and the economy, not a state-reading director.

## Integration hypotheses & what won't transfer *(graduating)*

- **The draft is the liftable core, the real-time sim is not.** Against the Storm recombines
  buildings and cornerstones across a live economy, so the draft-and-interact structure lifts into the
  LobCorp abnormality draft while the moment-to-moment settlement pacing does not, because LobCorp
  resolves work day by day rather than in real time.
- **Glade events are the legible-affix template.** Every glade couples its procedural reward to a
  telegraphed risk, which is the "fair means legible" bar (the Into the Breach rule) a rerolled
  abnormality affix has to clear. What won't transfer is the forest-biome spatial framing, because
  LobCorp has no generated map, so the reward-behind-telegraphed-risk shape lifts without the terrain.
